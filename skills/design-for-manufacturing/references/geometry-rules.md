# Manufacturable geometry

Define datums and tolerance only functional relationships. Use clearance and fits from an explicit standard and account for coating or treatment buildup.

Check process-specific access and risk: cutter reach and internal radii for machining; bend radii, reliefs, and flat-pattern feasibility for sheet metal; draft, uniform walls, gates, and ejection for molding; support, anisotropy, and post-processing for additive; fixturing and distortion for weldments.

Every critical feature needs a plausible inspection method and access path.
