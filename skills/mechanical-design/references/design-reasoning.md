# Design reasoning

Start from functions and boundary conditions. Identify load paths, supports, motion, environmental exposure, interfaces, and credible misuse. Draw a free-body diagram or equivalent force/moment accounting before sizing load-bearing features.

Choose materials from stiffness, strength, fatigue, temperature, corrosion, wear, mass, process, and availability together. State allowables and safety-factor basis; do not mix yield, ultimate, and fatigue limits.

Make dimensions traceable to a requirement, interface, calculation, standard, or manufacturing rule. Keep assumptions explicit so a changed requirement can invalidate the right decisions.
