# Workflow

Use this order: source inspection → blockout → camera → broad light → geometry cleanup → materials → refined light → details → preview critique → final render → restrained composite.

Keep presentation effort proportional:

- ordinary review: one or two stills;
- product presentation: hero, detail, and optional exploded still;
- animation: only when requested or when motion itself is the subject.

For moving assemblies, derive pivots and parentage from CAD joints. At rest, compare every part's world transform with the imported CAD state. Render rest, maximum travel, and one intermediate frame before starting a sequence.

For exploded views, keep a readable axis and spacing, retain at least one anchored part, and reveal internal order without scattering hardware outside the frame.

Critique previews by layer. Fix structural separation before look development. Fix camera before adding detail. Fix highlight shape with light size and position before changing metal color.
